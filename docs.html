How to use the troop calculator

The walkthrough below covers troop costs, inventory and speedups.

Start the walkthrough ↓
QUICK START

From troop count to complete plan

1

Choose an action

Select Train, Heal, or Promote at the top of the calculator.

2

Pick a tier

Choose the troop tier or promotion path you want to calculate.

3

Enter your values

Add troop count, speed bonus, resource reduction, and capacity.

4

Read your totals

Review resources, might, time, batches, and the time your bonus saves.

5

Plan your inventory

Optionally add current resources and speedups to check what is possible.

6

Share the result

Use Copy plan to paste a clear summary into alliance chat or Discord.

STEP 1

Choose the action

The selected action controls which costs and timings are used.

⚔ Train

Calculates the full cost, might, and time for creating new troops.

✦ Heal

Calculates the resources and time needed to restore wounded troops.

↑ Promote

Calculates the additional resources, time, and might gained when upgrading troops.

STEPS 2–3

Select a tier and enter your city values

Troop tier

Training and healing include T1–T10. Promotion supports any lower-to-higher tier pair through T10, including direct jumps.

Number of troops

The exact number you intend to train, heal, or promote. It can be left empty while setting up the other fields.

Training or healing speed

Your total percentage bonus for the selected activity. Enter 0 if you have no bonus.

Resource reduction

Any percentage reduction that applies to the resource cost. Enter 0 when none applies.

Capacity

The number of troops processed per batch. The default is 4,000 for City Hall 25, but it can be replaced with your city's value.

STEP 4

Understand your results

Food, wood, stone & gold

Total resources after any entered reduction.

Total might

Might represented by trained or healed troops, or might gained from promotion.

Original time

The total base duration before your speed bonus is applied.

Estimated time

The adjusted duration after applying the selected activity speed.

Total batches

The number of separate batches required at the entered capacity.

Time saved

The difference between original and estimated time.

STEP 5 · OPTIONAL

Inventory & speedup planner

Expand this section when you want to go beyond totals and check whether the plan is achievable with your current inventory.

Blank and zero are different

Leave a field blank when its balance is unknown. Enter 0 when the balance is known and you have none.

  1. Enter available resources. Each resource is marked Covered or shows the amount missing.
  2. Add general speedups. These are combined with the matching activity speedups.
  3. Add training or healing speedups. The label changes with the selected action.
  4. Review maximum possible. This uses only the balances you entered, so omitted resources do not limit the estimate.
  5. Check expected completion. This estimates the finish date after entered speedups are consumed.

HOW IT IS CALCULATED

The formulas behind the result

Resourcesbase cost × troop count × (1 − reduction ÷ 100)

Estimated timeoriginal time ÷ (1 + speed ÷ 100)

Batchesround up (troop count ÷ capacity)

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Useful details

Do speedups reduce resource costs?

No. Speedups reduce remaining time only. Resource reduction is entered separately.

Why might the in-game result differ slightly?

Game updates, unentered bonuses, event effects, and rounding can affect the final value. Check the current in-game preview before committing a large queue.

How do I see the guided tour again?

Return to the calculator and select How to use above the main panel.

Can I share a calculation?

Yes. Select Copy plan in the results panel, then paste the summary into Discord or alliance chat.
