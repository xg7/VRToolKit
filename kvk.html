Before you enter

Check eligibility

Join a tribe and check the entry screen for your Hall requirement and eligible heroes.

Follow Chronicles

Map objectives and routes unlock progressively. Use the timers for your current event.

Prepare, fight, settle

Preparation develops bonuses; battles determine control and merit; settlement distributes rewards.

Read the battle rules before committing troops

Central objectives and catapult fire can cause troop deaths instead of wounded troops. Confirm the rules of the specific building before joining an assault.
