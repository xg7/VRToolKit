SEASON 2 · ENGLAND

Expedition to England

Expand through English regions toward Windsor. Preparation buffs become active during war.

Entry
Chief’s Hall 22
Format
8 kingdoms
Final objective
Windsor Castle
ProgressionBuildingsMechanicsRewards
THE CAMPAIGN

Season progression

  1. Legion and Kingdom Fortresses open first.
  2. Passes, Monastery and successively stronger castles unlock.
  3. The Windsor zone opens before the final castle contest.

Use your in-game Chronicles for exact unlocks and timings.

KNOW THE RULES

Key mechanics

  • Potion Healing and Blessing Totem upgrades support wounded troops.
  • Glorious Iron Cavalry requires rallies; Rune Griffins drop temporary buffs.
  • Clear defender support and defeat the Royal Guard Captain before Windsor. Severely wounded troops there die.
MAP REFERENCE

Buildings & objectives

Kingdom Fortress

Rebuild with materials, then hero shards for Honor Coins; unlock territory relocation.

Legion Fortress

Early capture objective providing buffs.

Bastion / Fort / Sacred Castle

Level 5–7 objectives with scheduled capture windows.

Manors

Level 4–8 properties; check occupation rules in-game.

Passes / Ports

Control land access and river routes.

Bloody Monastery / Abandoned Temple

Monster battlefields; the Temple has stronger enemies.

Catapult Towers

Surround Windsor and inflict troop deaths on its defenders.

SETTLEMENT

Victory & rewards

Windsor ownership, tribe achievements and personal rank influence victory rewards and Expedition Summon attempts.

Check the event’s reward requirements and claim windows before settlement.
