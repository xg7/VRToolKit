SEASON 8 · MUSPELHEIM

Surtr’s Flames

The Legion of Bounty faces the Allied Legion in a territory system built around captured cities.

Entry
Check season entry screen
Format
2 legions · balanced or asymmetric
Final objective
Surtr, the Fire Jotun
ProgressionBuildingsMechanicsRewards
THE CAMPAIGN

Season progression

  1. Donate Frost Tears; elect captains and assign starting regions.
  2. City contests and breakthroughs unlock stronger ruins.
  3. Surtr’s Hand, Spine and Mouth lead toward the central level 10 objective.

Use your in-game Chronicles for exact unlocks and timings.

KNOW THE RULES

Key mechanics

  • Tribe forts and flags cannot be built; capture cities and ruins to expand.
  • Battalions share rallies and reinforcements. Divine Awakening offers role-specific bonuses.
  • Preparation scoring uses a kingdom-count multiplier; asymmetric victories can increase Team rewards.
MAP REFERENCE

Buildings & objectives

Kingdom Fortress

Two repair stages provide buffs, supplies and territory colour.

Legion Fortress

Occupation yields points and stacking bonuses.

Surtr’s Hand / Spine / Mouth

Level 7–9 ruins provide Battalion buffs.

Strategic Cities / Chapels

Defeat the City Marshal and demolish defensive Chapels during sieges.

Hellish Forge / Abyss

Contested PvE battlefields for hero experience.

Team Airfields

Transport between legion locations.

Catapult Towers

Enemy towers kill Surtr’s defenders.

SETTLEMENT

Victory & rewards

Final control of Surtr determines victory and the occupying Battalion’s emblem.

Check the event’s reward requirements and claim windows before settlement.
