SEASON 5 · REALM OF TERROR

Hela’s Curse

Inferno, Boulder, Torrent and Void fight through alternating Armament and War periods.

THE CAMPAIGN

Season progression

  1. Capture and restore fortresses.
  2. Grim Church, Dead Forest and successive passes become available.
  3. Progress through level 5–7 castles toward the Palace.

Use your in-game Chronicles for exact unlocks and timings.

KNOW THE RULES

Key mechanics

  • Armament restricts attacks. Personal and Tribe strategies reset when periods change.
  • Donate Exorcist’s Bells during preparation.
  • Defeat the Cursed Guard Captain before attacking the Palace. Riders require rallies; Elusive Guardians are solo targets.
MAP REFERENCE

Buildings & objectives

Kingdom Fortress

Two-stage rebuilding restores kingdom effects and territory colour.

Legion Fortress

First capture benefits the Battalion.

Plague / Dilapidated / Silent Castles

Capture and cleanse for Battalion buffs.

Grim Church / Dead Forest

Drive enemies out of these PvE areas; Forest monsters are stronger.

Manors

Cursed Baron encounters cannot be rallied; occupation and chests are shared within the Battalion.

Airfields

Fast transfers for eligible troops.

Catapult Towers

Nearby troop numbers determine control; hostile towers kill Palace defenders.

SETTLEMENT

Victory & rewards

Final Palace control determines victory; castles and merit influence settlement rewards and seasonal summons.

Check the event’s reward requirements and claim windows before settlement.
